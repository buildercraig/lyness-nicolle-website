About us

A Salisbury building firm you can talk to

Lyness & Nicolle is run by builder Craig Nicolle. When you call, you speak to the person who'll be looking after your build — no call centres, no hand-offs.

Craig brings 25 years of building experience to every job. That includes two years working on Grade I and Grade II listed buildings, learning how to handle delicate traditional materials with the care they need — skills that make a real difference on older homes.
